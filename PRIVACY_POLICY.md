# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 12 October 2026**

Disk Partition Calculator is a utility for calculating partition sizes for Windows Setup. This policy explains what information the Android app handles, how it is stored, and when another service may be involved.

## Information handled by the app

Partition sizes you enter, calculation results, selected filesystem, Windows Setup profile, and app preferences are processed on your device. The app saves up to 100 recent calculation records and your preferences in its private app storage. It does not require an account or ask you to provide your name, email address, location, contacts, or other personal details.

## Collection and use

The developer does not operate a server for this app and does not receive your disk sizes, calculations, history, or preferences. Calculations run locally. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

## Google Play and sharing

The app may use Google Play's in-app update service to check whether an update is available, and the Rate Us action opens the Google Play listing. Google may process technical, app, or account information under its own privacy terms; the developer does not receive your calculation history through these features. Share App opens Android's share sheet with a static Play Store link. If you choose another app, that app receives the link and handles it under its own privacy policy. Your calculations and history are not attached to the shared link.

## Storage, backup, and retention

History and preferences are kept in app-private storage. You can remove individual history entries from the History sheet or clear all history in Settings. Preferences remain until changed or app data is cleared. Android backup and device transfer are allowed for this app's data; if enabled in your device settings, Android may back up, transfer, or restore history and preferences. The developer cannot access or control those backups.

## Security

App data is protected by Android's app sandbox. Any backup or device transfer is managed by Android and your device account settings. No method of storage or transfer can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Children

This is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer using the support email shown on this app's Google Play Store listing.
