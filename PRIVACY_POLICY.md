# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 13 October 2026**

This policy covers the **Disk Partition Calculator** Android app (package `com.diskcalc`). It is the same text shown in the app under Settings > Privacy Policy. Host this document at a public, non-editable URL (not a PDF) and link it in the Google Play Console and from the store listing.

## Summary

- Your calculations, history and preferences stay on your device.
- The app has no account, no sign-in and no developer-operated server.
- The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.
- The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
- The developer does not sell, rent or share your data.

## Developer and contact

The developer is the publisher named on this app's Google Play Store listing. For privacy questions, data requests or concerns, contact the developer at the support email address shown on the app's Google Play Store listing.

## Information the app handles

The following information is processed and stored only on your device:

- **Calculation inputs:** visible partition size in GB, total disk size in MB, partition count, filesystem, Windows Setup simulation on or off, and custom Windows Setup overhead.
- **Calculation results and history:** up to 100 most recent conversions, including the mode (Calc or Split), entered values, filesystem, result values, a text summary and a timestamp.
- **App preferences:** theme (Dark Mode or Light Mode), default filesystem, default Windows Setup simulation, selected Windows Setup profile and custom overhead value.

## Personal information

The app does not collect or ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising ID. It does not require an account.

## How the data is used

- To run partition-size calculations on your device.
- To show your saved history in the History sheet and let you copy or delete entries.
- To remember your preferences between app sessions.

The data is not used for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

## Sharing and third parties

The developer does not share your data with third parties. The following features involve other services only when you use them or when the app checks for updates:

- **Google Play:** the in-app update check is handled by Google Play. Rate Us opens the Play Store listing. Google processes information under its own privacy terms.
- **Android share sheet:** Share App sends only a static Play Store link to the app you choose. That app handles the link under its own privacy policy. Your calculations and history are not attached.
- **Android backup and device transfer:** if enabled in your device settings, Android may back up or transfer app data. The developer cannot access or control those backups.
- **Clipboard:** the Copy buttons place results on your clipboard only when you tap them. The app does not read the clipboard.

## Security

- History and preferences are kept in app-private storage, which Android's app sandbox keeps away from other apps.
- The app does not send your calculations, history or preferences over the network.
- Update checks and the Play Store listing are handled by Google Play over encrypted connections.
- No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Retention and deletion

- **History:** up to 100 entries are kept. When you save more, the oldest entry is removed automatically. History stays until you delete it.
- **Preferences:** kept until you change them or clear the app's data.
- **Delete one history entry:** open History and tap the delete icon on that entry.
- **Delete all history:** open Settings, then History, then Clear.
- **Delete all app data:** open Android Settings, then Apps, then Disk Partition Calculator, then Storage, then Clear storage. Uninstalling the app also removes its private data from the device.
- **Android backup:** if backup is enabled, a copy may exist in your device or Google backup. You can manage or delete it in your device backup settings.

The developer does not hold copies of your data, so there is nothing on the developer's side to request or delete. The app has no account, so there is no account to delete.

## Children

This is a general-purpose utility for Windows users and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your choices

- You can view, delete or change your history and preferences at any time inside the app.
- You can turn off Android backup for this app in your device settings.
- You can stop using the app and uninstall it at any time.
- For questions about rights that apply to you under local privacy laws, contact the developer using the details in the Developer and contact section.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The version in the app is the current one, and the date at the top shows when it was last updated.
