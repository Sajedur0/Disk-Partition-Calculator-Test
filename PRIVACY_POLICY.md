# Privacy Policy for Disk Partition Calculator

**Last Updated: 26 October 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how the Disk Partition Calculator app ("the App") handles information when you use our services.

## Summary

- Your calculations, history, and preferences stay **only on your device**
- The app has **no account, no sign-in, and no developer-operated server**
- The app requests **no runtime permissions**. The only network permission is `INTERNET`, used
  solely by Google Play's flexible in-app update check; your files, location and hardware are
  never accessed
- The app does **not** ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files
- The app contains **no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs** — the only
  Google library is Play In-App Updates
- The developer does **not** sell, rent, or share your data with anyone

## Developer and Contact

This policy covers the Disk Partition Calculator Android app (package: `com.diskcalc`). The developer is the publisher named on the app's Google Play Store listing.

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns, please contact the developer at the support email address shown on the app's Google Play Store listing.

## Information the App Handles

The following information is processed and stored **only on your device**:

### Calculation Inputs
- Visible partition size in GB
- Total disk size in MB
- Partition count
- Filesystem type (NTFS or FAT32)
- Windows Setup simulation status (on/off)
- Custom Windows Setup overhead value

### Calculation Results and History
- Up to 100 most recent conversions
- Mode (Calc or Split)
- Entered values
- Filesystem type
- Result values
- Text summary
- Timestamp
- Partition count (Split results only)
- A randomly generated identifier (UUID) for each entry, used only to identify it in the list.
  It is created by the app on your device, is **not** a device or advertising identifier, and has no
  meaning outside the app

### App Preferences
- Theme selection (System, Light or Dark)
- Default filesystem
- Default Windows Setup simulation status
- Selected Windows Setup profile
- Custom overhead value

## Personal Information

The app does **not** collect or ask for:
- Your name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Location
- Contacts
- Photos
- Device identifiers
- Advertising ID

The app does **not** require an account or any form of registration.

## How the Data is Used

The data mentioned above is used solely for the following purposes:

- To run partition-size calculations on your device
- To show your saved history in the History sheet
- To let you copy or delete history entries
- To remember your preferences between app sessions

**The data is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any other commercial purposes.**

## Data Sharing and Third Parties

The developer does **not** share your data with third parties.

The following features involve other services **only when you explicitly use them**:

### Google Play Services
- The app uses **Google Play In-App Updates** (flexible flow with Play's native UI) to check
  whether a newer version is available and, if you accept, to download and install it automatically
- The update check sends **no** calculation, history or preference data — it only asks Play about
  a newer app version
- The "Rate Us" button opens the Play Store listing
- Google processes those interactions under its own privacy policy

Apart from the AndroidX/Jetpack libraries and Google Play In-App Updates, the app bundles **no
other third-party SDKs** — no advertising, analytics, attribution, crash-reporting, or
social-media SDK is included.

### Android Share Sheet
- The "Share App" feature sends only a static Play Store link to the app you choose
- That app handles the link under its own privacy policy
- Your calculations and history are **not** attached or shared

### Android Backup
- The app opts out of Android backup (`allowBackup="false"`)
- History and preferences are **not** backed up or transferred to another device

### Clipboard
- The Copy buttons place calculation results on your clipboard only when you tap them
- The app does **not** read from the clipboard or access any other clipboard content

## Security

- History and preferences are kept in app-private storage, which Android's app sandbox keeps away from other apps
- The app does **not** send your calculations, history, or preferences over the network
- The app contains **no cloud-sync code**; the only network code is the Google Play in-app
  update check described above
- No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure

## Data Retention and Deletion

### History
- Up to 100 entries are kept
- When you save more, the oldest entry is removed automatically
- History stays until you delete it

### Preferences
- Kept until you change them or clear the app's data

### Delete Options
- **Delete one history entry**: Open History and tap the delete icon on that entry
- **Delete all history**: Open Settings > History > Clear and confirm the dialog
- **Delete all app data**: Open Android Settings > Apps > Disk Partition Calculator > Storage > Clear storage
- **Uninstall**: Uninstalling the app also removes its private data from the device
- **Android backup**: The app opts out of backup, so no copy exists in device or Google backups

The developer does **not** hold copies of your data, so there is nothing on the developer's side to request or delete. The app has no account, so there is no account to delete.

## Children

This is a general-purpose utility for Windows users and is **not** directed to children. The app does **not** knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your Choices

You can:
- View, delete, or change your history and preferences at any time inside the app
- No backup is created — Android backup is disabled for this app, so nothing leaves the device
- Stop using the app and uninstall it at any time
- For questions about rights that apply to you under local privacy laws, contact the developer using the details in the Developer and Contact section

Because the app does not collect personal information, the developer holds **no personal data about
you** to access, correct, export, or delete, and there is nothing to opt out of.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The version in the app is the current one, and the date at the top shows when it was last updated.
